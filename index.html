About CodeRave 2.0

The flagship hackathon organized by Internwell SLIET and hosted on Devfolio on 09–10 February 2027.

SLIET Longowal Campus • 451 Acres Lush Green Hub

CodeRave 2.0 is organized by Internwell SLIET and hosted nationwide on Devfolio on 09–10 February 2027. The tournament features two distinct competition pathways: an Online Track open across India where hackers submit their Project Idea, and an exclusive On-Campus Offline Track for SLIET students featuring Day 1 PPT Screening followed by an intensive 24-Hour live prototype build sprint and live stage demo before our industry jury.

Dual Track Model

Online nationwide track on Devfolio & exclusive on-campus 24H arena for SLIET engineers.

Industry Guidance

100% Free Entry

Zero registration fees for both Online Devfolio participants and Offline SLIET students.

Upcoming Event: CodeRave 2.0

09th – 10th February, 2027 • Hosted on Devfolio • Online & Offline for SLIET Students.

PHASE 01 • DAY 1

Idea Screening & Shortlisting

09 February 2027 • 09:00 AM IST

Online Track: Submit your Project Idea on the Devfolio portal.
Offline Track (SLIET): Teams present their idea PPT on campus for screening and arena shortlisting.

  • Registration & verification via Devfolio
  • Online: Project idea evaluation
  • Offline: PPT presentation on SLIET campus
PHASE 02 • DAY 2

Prototype Arena (24H Sprint)

10 February 2027 • 24 Hours Non-Stop

Shortlisted offline SLIET teams enter the adrenaline-filled 24-hour live coding marathon in the arena to convert approved PPT ideas into a live functional demo.

  • 24 continuous hours of live build sprint
  • Midnight mentorship & debugging sessions
  • Full functional working prototype deployment
PHASE 03 • FINALE

Live Pitch & Distinct Winners

10 February 2027 Evening • Grand Jury

Offline teams demo working prototypes live on stage before Pine Labs engineers. Crowning 1st, 2nd, and 3rd offline prizes and 1st & 2nd online Devfolio prizes!

  • Offline Live Stage Demo & Architecture Defense
  • Q&A with Pine Labs evaluation jury
  • Distinct Online (1st & 2nd) & Offline (1st, 2nd, 3rd) awards

Dual Tracks & Working Model

Hosted on Devfolio with dedicated pathways for Online nationwide hackers and Offline SLIET champions.

STEP 01

Apply on Devfolio

Register your team through our official Devfolio portal and choose either the Online Track or Offline Track (SLIET students).

STEP 02

Online Idea / Offline PPT

Online: Give your Project Idea & architecture.
Offline: Give your PPT presentation on Day 1 at SLIET.

STEP 03

24H Live Sprint (Offline)

Shortlisted SLIET teams enter the 24-hour continuous arena marathon to transform concepts into working code.

STEP 04

Live Demo & Crown Winners

Give live prototype demos on stage in 24 hours to win 1st, 2nd, 3rd (Offline) and 1st, 2nd (Online)!
