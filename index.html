INSTITUTION & COMMUNITY

About SLIET & Our Organizers

Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology (SLIET) — Empowering engineering leaders since 1989 across a 451-acre campus.

451 Acres Green Campus

"Education is the passport to the future, for tomorrow belongs to those who prepare for it today."

— SLIET Deemed-to-be-University

Centrally Funded Technical Institute (CFTI)

Established in 1989 by the Ministry of Education (MoE), SLIET is accredited with Grade 'A' by NAAC. Offering multi-entry and multi-exit technical programs under NEP-2020.

Internwell SLIET & SSDC Synergy

Internwell drives student upskilling, internships, and startup readiness. Together with SSDC (SLIET Software Development Club), CodeRave 2.0 provides an exclusive arena for college peers to code and shine.

Past Legacy: Coding Spardha Precedent

Our team previously organized Coding Spardha with 150+ teams and over 2,000+ student footfall. CodeRave 2.0 continues this spirit with a structured 2-day format.

2-DAY TOURNAMENT STRUCTURE

How CodeRave 2.0 Works

From initial PPT concept submission to intense 24-hour demo development and final jury pitch.

ROUND 01

PPT Submission Round

28th November, 2026 (Day 1)

Teams submit their project presentation deck. Submissions are accepted both online via portal and offline at the SLIET campus desk. Cover your problem statement, solution architecture, tech stack, and impact.

  • Online PPT portal upload option
  • Offline physical deck submission at SLIET
  • Screening & shortlisting evaluation
ROUND 02

24-Hour Live Demo Build

29th November, 2026 (Day 2)

Shortlisted teams enter the 24-Hour Live Demo Round. You get a continuous 24-hour window to implement the proposed codebase, deploy a live functional prototype, and prep demo presentations.

  • Shortlisted teams announced
  • 24 Hours of continuous building
  • Mentorship check-ins by SSDC seniors
FINALE

Live Demo Pitch & Winners

29th November Evening

Teams pitch their live working prototypes directly in front of the judge panel. Working software, design polish, and clarity of answers determine the champions of CodeRave 2.0!

  • Live Demo execution & Q&A
  • Jury evaluation & score tabulation
  • Winner announcement & prize honours

COMMON QUERIES

Frequently Asked Questions

Key details about PPT submission, the 24-hour demo round, and eligibility.

CodeRave 2.0 is organized exclusively for students of SLIET (Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology) across all departments, years, diploma, and degree programs.

On 28 November 2026, teams can submit their idea presentation deck either online through our portal or offline at the SLIET campus registration desk. Both modes are equally evaluated!

Teams shortlisted from the PPT round are invited to the 24-Hour Live Demo phase on 29 November 2026. Teams have a continuous 24-hour sprint to implement and demonstrate a functional prototype before the judges.

Teams can have between 1 to 4 members. All team members must be enrolled students of SLIET.

Zero fee! CodeRave 2.0 is 100% free of charge for all participating SLIET students.

The full prize pool breakdown and category awards are currently being finalized and will be unveiled ahead of the event.
