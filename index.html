About CodeRave 2.0

SLIET's flagship collegiate hackathon organized exclusively by Internwell to inspire student developers and engineers.

SLIET Longowal Campus • 451 Acres Lush Green Hub

CodeRave 2.0 is organized exclusively for the students of Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET) by Internwell. Participants register exclusively via the official Hackathon link to qualify for Day 1 idea screening. Shortlisted teams are then granted 24 continuous hours in the arena to construct a live functional prototype and demo it before our industry judges.

Campus Innovation

Empowering SLIET engineering minds to turn ambitious ideas into viable tech solutions.

24H Live Sprint

Shortlisted teams enter an adrenaline-filled 24-hour continuous prototype build sprint.

Industry Guidance

Direct evaluation and mentorship from seasoned software engineers at Pine Labs & alumni.

100% Free & Exclusive

Zero registration fees, exclusive to SLIET students across all degrees and branches.

Upcoming Event: CodeRave 2.0

28th – 29th November, 2026 • The premier 2-Day Hackathon exclusively for SLIET students.

PHASE 01 • DAY 1

Idea Screening & Shortlisting

28 November 2026 • 09:00 AM IST

Registered teams present their ideas to the evaluation panel on SLIET campus for feasibility review, architecture scrutiny, and finalist shortlisting.

  • Registered team verification via Hackathon link
  • On-campus problem statement evaluation
  • Finalist shortlisting for the 24H arena
PHASE 02 • DAY 2

Prototype Arena (24H Sprint)

29 November 2026 • 24 Hours Non-Stop

Shortlisted teams enter the 24-hour live coding marathon to transform their approved ideas into a working live demo.

  • 24 continuous hours of live development
  • Midnight mentorship & debugging support
  • Full working prototype deployment
PHASE 03 • FINALE

Live Pitch & Winner Decided

29 November 2026 Evening • Grand Jury

Finalists demonstrate working code on stage before our distinguished jury, including Pine Labs software engineers and faculty.

  • Live stage demo & architecture defence
  • Q&A with Pine Labs evaluation jury
  • Champion crowning & award reveal

Working Model
