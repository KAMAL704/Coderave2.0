What's CodeRave 2.0?

CodeRave 2.0 is the annual flagship hackathon organized exclusively for students of Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET) by Internwell. We bring together student innovators from all years and departments to pitch ideas, code non-stop, and build working technology solutions that solve real campus and societal problems.

What's going down

CodeRave 2.0 operates in a structured two-stage tournament format. Day 1 features PPT deck submission (both online and offline on campus), followed by a 24-hour live coding sprint on Day 2 for shortlisted teams to create working demos and pitch to the jury!

STAGE 01

PPT Submission Round

28th November, 2026 (Day 1)

Teams present their project blueprints. Submit your pitch deck either online via the portal or offline at the SLIET registration desk.

  • Online portal upload option
  • Offline submission on SLIET campus
  • Screening & shortlist announcement
STAGE 02

24-Hour Live Demo Build

29th November, 2026 (Day 2)

Shortlisted finalist teams receive a non-stop 24-hour window to code, debug, and deploy their functional working prototypes.

  • 24 hours of continuous engineering
  • Mentorship check-ins from seniors
  • Deployment verification
FINALE

Live Pitching & Winners

29th November Evening

Teams demonstrate their live running code directly before the judges. Working software quality and presentation decide the champions!

  • Live product demo execution
  • Q&A and technical evaluation
  • Winner trophies & honours

FAQ

Who can attend CodeRave 2.0?

CodeRave 2.0 is organized exclusively for students of SLIET (Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology) across all departments, years, diploma, and degree programs.

How much does CodeRave cost?

Admission is completely free! There is zero registration fee for all SLIET students.

How does Day 1 PPT submission work?

On 28 November 2026, teams can submit their presentation deck either online through our portal or offline at the SLIET registration desk. Both formats are equally evaluated.

What is the Day 2 24-hour live demo round?

Shortlisted teams get 24 continuous hours to build a working prototype of their proposed project, which is then pitched live to the jury.

How big are the teams?

Teams may consist of 1 to 4 members. All team members must be enrolled students of SLIET.

Are prizes announced?

The specific prize pool numbers and categories will be unveiled closer to the hackathon launch.

What if I am new to hackathons?

Everyone is welcome! Mentors and organizers will be available throughout to guide you on software design and presentation.
