About Coderave 2.0

National-Level Hybrid Hackathon • Organized by Internwell SLIET Club • 13–14 February 2027

Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET), Longowal, Punjab

Coderave 2.0 is a national-level hybrid hackathon designed to bring together innovative minds, passionate developers, designers, and problem-solvers to transform ideas into impactful technology solutions.

Organized by Internwell SLIET Club, this edition offers students an opportunity to demonstrate their creativity, technical expertise, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities through a competitive, time-bound development experience.

The hackathon welcomes teams from colleges and universities across India through its Online Participation Track, while SLIET students can participate in the Offline Campus Track. Shortlisted teams will present their ideas through PPT pitches, followed by an intensive 24-hour open-source development challenge in which participants will build, test, and demonstrate their solutions.

Online Track (All India)

Pan-India participation with remote PPT pitches and 24-hour sprint for top shortlisted finalists.

Offline Track (SLIET)

Exclusive on-campus 24H live marathon at SLIET Longowal, Punjab from 13 Feb 5 PM to 14 Feb 5 PM.

Inclusive Diversity

Mandatory requirement of at least 1 girl member per registered team (max 3 girls).

No PS & Domain Limit

Open Innovation across all domains. All teams compete for unified 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Grand Prizes.

What Does Internwell SLIET Club Do?

Internwell SLIET Club is the premier technical, innovation, and career-advancement community at Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering and Technology (SLIET). Operating at the nexus of technology and industry, Internwell empowers undergraduate and diploma students to bridge the gap between classroom theory and industry-grade engineering.

Student Entrepreneurship & Incubation

Fostering student startup culture from ideation to venture building, pitch deck refinement, patent guidance, and university incubation support.

Tech Bootcamps & Skill Acceleration

Organizing hands-on coding bootcamps covering modern Full-Stack development, AI/ML, Cloud computing, Cybersecurity, and open-source contribution.

Landmark Competitions

Pioneering premier competitive tech events like CodeRave (2000+ footfall) and Coding Spardha that elevate SLIET's technical prestige nationwide.

Industry Mentorship & Placement Fast-Track

Connecting SLIET innovators directly with senior software engineers, industry leaders, alumni networks, internship opportunities, and career referrals.

Participation Tracks

Coderave 2.0 offers two competition pathways competing for unified 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Prizes: an Online Track for teams across India and an On-Campus Offline Track for SLIET students. Open Innovation • No PS & Domain Limit!

ONLINE TRACK • ALL INDIA (DEVFOLIO)

Online Participation Track

Pan-India Remote Hackathon Experience

Open to eligible student teams from colleges and universities across India via online registration on Devfolio.
Pan-India Eligibility: Open to active undergraduate and diploma student teams from recognized colleges across India.
Proposal Submission: Register online on Devfolio and submit your project proposal PPT (Open Innovation • Any Tech Domain).
National Shortlist: A maximum of 20 teams will be shortlisted nationally for the online evaluation track.
Online PPT Pitch: Shortlisted teams pitch their idea and technical architecture virtually to our expert evaluation jury.
'Prototype Arena' Sprint: Top 5 teams participate in the 24-hour 'Prototype Arena' challenge remotely from their preferred location.
Deliverables: Submit functional source code repository, documentation, and demonstrable demo video.
Campus Invitation: Online participants may receive invites to attend the offline campus event if feasible.
Unified Grand Podium: Competes directly for the unified Hackathon 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Grand Prizes!
REGISTER ON DEVFOLIO (ONLINE)
OFFLINE TRACK • SLIET CAMPUS

Offline Campus Track

24-Hour Live Arena at SLIET Longowal

Exclusive on-campus track for students of SLIET, Longowal at the lush green 451-acre campus.
SLIET Eligibility: Open exclusively to bona fide undergraduate and diploma students of SLIET, Longowal.
Proposal Submission: Register online and submit your project proposal PPT during registration (No PS & No Domain limit).
'Idea Forces' Campus Pitch: Shortlisted teams pitch PPT in person on campus on 13 February in the 'Idea Forces' defense.
'Prototype Arena' 24H Live: On-campus live marathon begins at 5:00 PM on 13 Feb and concludes at 5:00 PM on 14 Feb.
Live Implementation: Teams build and test functional solutions with live mentorship, lab setups, and open-source standards.
Stage Defense: Live onstage prototype demonstration and technical defense before industry jury and faculty evaluators.
Campus Hub: Refreshments, networking with industry mentors, official hacker swags, and hackathon certificates.
Unified Grand Podium: Competes directly for the unified Hackathon 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Grand Prizes!

Team Formation & Eligibility

Official team composition criteria and mandatory requirements announced by the organizers.

Core Requirement

Team Size: 3 to 6 Members

Each team must have a minimum of 3 members and a maximum of 6 members. Solo participation is not permitted to encourage teamwork and diverse technical capabilities.

MANDATORY DIVERSITY RULE

Mandatory Girl Member

Each team must include at least 1 girl member. A maximum of 3 girl members is permitted per team. Applications without at least one female teammate will not be eligible.

Single Point of Contact

Designated Team Leader

One member must be designated as the Team Leader. The Team Leader will complete the registration on behalf of the entire team and provide accurate details for every member.

Student Status

Single Team & Eligibility

Each participant may belong to only one registered team. All team members must be active students of recognized colleges or universities and meet the eligibility requirements announced by the organizers.

Important Registration Notice:
The Team Leader completes registration on behalf of the entire team. Please ensure all member contact numbers, emails, and college credentials are accurate.

Registration & Selection Process

A transparent 6-round journey from initial proposal pitch to the 24-hour sprint and winner announcement.

ROUND 01 Initial Entry

Registration & PPT Submission

The designated Team Leader registers the complete team on the hackathon portal and submits the project proposal PPT outlining problem statement, proposed architecture, and tech stack.

  • Team Leader completes registration
  • Complete member details submitted
  • Project proposal PPT uploaded
ROUND 02 Expert Screening

PPT Evaluation & Shortlisting

Judges rigorously evaluate submissions based on innovation, problem relevance, feasibility, technical approach, potential impact, and presentation quality to identify top ideas.

  • Innovation & problem relevance
  • Feasibility & technical approach
  • Potential impact & presentation
ROUND 03 'Idea Forces' Pitch

'Idea Forces' — PPT Pitch & Screening

Shortlisted online teams (maximum 20 teams) pitch to judges online. Shortlisted SLIET teams present in the 'Idea Forces' offline campus pitch session on 13 February.

  • 'Idea Forces' SLIET pitch on campus (13 Feb)
  • Online: Max 20 shortlisted teams pitch
  • Live architecture & feasibility jury Q&A
ROUND 04 24-Hour Marathon

'Prototype Arena' — 24-Hour Hackathon

Finalist teams enter the 'Prototype Arena' — building, testing, and shipping demonstrable open-source software within 24 continuous hours (13 Feb 5 PM – 14 Feb 5 PM). No PS limit and No domain limit!

  • 13 Feb 5:00 PM to 14 Feb 5:00 PM
  • Open Innovation: No PS / Domain limit
  • Offline on campus + Online top 5 remote
ROUND 05 Code & Demo

Final Submission & Evaluation

Teams submit their working solution, source code repository, project documentation, and demo video. Judges evaluate code quality, functionality, open-source adherence, and final live demos.

  • Git repository & commit history
  • Project documentation & demo video
  • Live demo verification before grand jury
ROUND 06 Victory Ceremony

Grand Winner Announcement (1st, 2nd, 3rd)

Official declaration of the unified hackathon podium: 1st Prize Winner 🥇, 2nd Prize Winner 🥈, and 3rd Prize Winner 🥉, celebrated with trophies, cash prizes, certificates, and Internwell placement fast-tracks.

  • 1st Prize Winner 🥇 (Grand Champion)
  • 2nd Prize Winner 🥈 & 3rd Prize Winner 🥉
  • Prize amounts revealed at 'Reignition' (06 Feb)
CORE 24H SPRINT • NO PS & DOMAIN LIMIT

'Prototype Arena' — 24-Hour Development Challenge

Turn innovative ideas into demonstrable, functional prototypes in 24 continuous hours. Complete creative & domain freedom!

13 Feb, 5:00 PM – 14 Feb, 5:00 PM

Open-Source Technologies

Teams are expected to develop their solutions during the official event window and use open-source tools, libraries, and frameworks wherever possible.

Repository & Documentation

Participants must maintain a project repository, provide clear and comprehensive documentation, and ensure that their software work is fully demonstrable.

License & Disclosures

Teams must disclose all third-party components and follow applicable open-source licences (e.g., MIT, Apache 2.0, GPL).

Originality & Prior Work

Previously developed work must be explicitly declared, and the core solution built during the sprint must comply with the organizers' originality requirements.

Applies to both Online finalists (Top 5 remote teams) and Offline SLIET campus participants.
