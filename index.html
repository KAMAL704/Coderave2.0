INSTITUTION & ROOTS

About SLIET & Our Legacy

Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET) — Pioneering technical excellence, multi-tier research, and student innovation since 1989.

451 Acres Lush Green Campus

"Education is the passport to the future, for tomorrow belongs to those who prepare for it today."

— Official SLIET Motto

Centrally Funded Technical Institute (CFTI)

Established in 1989 by the Ministry of Education (MoE), Govt. of India, SLIET is a premier Deemed-to-be-University accredited with Grade 'A' by NAAC. Programs are NBA-accredited following the visionary multi-entry and multi-exit NEP-2020 framework.

Organized by Internwell & SSDC

Internwell spearheads professional upskilling, internship opportunities, and entrepreneurial guidance. In synergy with SSDC (SLIET Software Development Club), the university's elite coding society, CodeRave provides a launchpad for future technological leaders.

PROVEN TRACK RECORD

The "Coding Spardha" Precedent

On February 2, 2023, the team conducted Coding Spardha — an electrifying one-day extravaganza that drew over 150+ teams, a staggering 2,000+ footfall, and masterclasses by seasoned industry experts. CodeRave 2.0 scales this foundation into an epic 36-hour nationwide arena.

TIMELINE OF EVENTS

The 36-Hour Schedule

Synchronized timeline of CodeRave 2.0 (April 12th - 14th, 2024). Explore events, milestone reviews, and midnight mini-games.

04:00 PM

Check-In & Team Registration

Arrival at SLIET Main Auditorium & virtual Discord check-in for hybrid teams. Distribution of participant badge kits.

05:30 PM

Grand Opening Ceremony & Keynotes

Welcome address by SLIET Faculty Mentors, Internwell & SSDC leaders. Keynote session on software scaling and innovation.

07:00 PM
HACKING COMMENCES

Hacking Clock Starts (Hour 00:00)

Problem statement deep dive. Teams spin up GitHub repos, configure development branches, and initiate codebase architecture.

10:30 PM

Dinner & Networking Lounge

Fuel up for the night. Informal discussions with senior developers and mentors.

02:00 AM

Midnight Mini-Games & Energy Hour

Trivia quiz, typing speed battles, and energizing music to keep the creative adrenaline pumping!

09:00 AM

Mentor Checkpoint #1 (Architecture Review)

Expert mentors and Pine Labs engineers review prototypes, inspect API hooks, and provide architectural optimizations.

03:30 PM

Technical Workshop: Web3 & AI Micro-services

Special technical breakout session hosted by our community sponsors.

09:00 PM

Mentor Checkpoint #2 (Feature Freeze Review)

Pre-evaluation review to verify working MVP deliverables and validate deployment links.

07:00 AM
PENS DOWN

Final Submissions on Devfolio (Hour 36:00)

Code freeze. All commits, demo video links, and project descriptions must be locked in on the Devfolio portal.

09:30 AM

Judging & Final Demo Pitching

Top qualifying teams pitch their live creations before the esteemed jury panel including Vaishnavi P. (Pine Labs).

02:30 PM

Award Ceremony & Closing Celebration

Announcement of Grand Champions, Track Winners, trophy distribution, and closing address by Internwell SLIET.

GOT QUESTIONS?

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about participating, team limits, submissions, and on-site logistics.

You can participate in teams ranging from 1 to 4 members. Solo hackers are welcome, or you can find team members via our official Discord community channels!

Nada. 100% Free! There is absolutely zero registration cost to participate in CodeRave 2.0. Our generous partners and sponsors cover everything.

Any enrolled student from undergraduate, postgraduate, or diploma programs across engineering, science, or design colleges nationwide is eligible. Beginners, seasoned open-source contributors, and first-time hackathon attendees are all celebrated!

You are in the perfect place! CodeRave provides continuous mentorship sessions, workshops on building scalable apps, and round-the-clock technical guidance from SSDC and industry mentors to help you ship your dream project.

CodeRave follows a hybrid structure. The online portal runs via Devfolio allowing teams nationwide to participate remotely, while participants situated around SLIET can join live at the Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology campus hub.

CodeRave follows the official Devfolio Code of Conduct. We are dedicated to providing a safe, respectful, harassment-free, and inclusive environment for everyone.
