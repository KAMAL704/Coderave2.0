About CodeRave 2.0

SLIET's flagship collegiate hackathon organized exclusively by Internwell to inspire the next generation of engineers and problem-solvers.

SLIET Longowal Campus • 451 Acres Lush Green Hub

CodeRave 2.0 is organized exclusively for the students of Sant Longowal Institute of Engineering & Technology (SLIET) by Internwell. The event features a distinct two-phase format: on Day 1, students submit their idea PPTs (both Online and Offline on campus) for jury shortlisting. Selected teams are granted 24 continuous hours to construct a live functional prototype and demo it before our industry judges.

Campus Innovation

Empowering SLIET engineering minds to turn ambitious ideas into viable tech solutions.

24H Live Sprint

Industry Guidance

Direct evaluation and mentorship from seasoned software engineers at Pine Labs & alumni.

100% Free & Exclusive

Zero registration fees, exclusive to SLIET students across all degrees and branches.

Working Model

The complete four-step trajectory from PPT submission to winning CodeRave 2.0.

STEP 01

Submit PPT (Online & Offline)

Submit your deck online via the submission tab or drop it physically at the SLIET campus desk on Day 1.

STEP 02

Jury Shortlisting

Our review panel inspects feasibility, technical ambition, and campus impact to shortlist top finalists.

STEP 03

24-Hour Live Demo Sprint

Shortlisted teams get 24 continuous hours in the arena to convert their slides into a working live demo.

STEP 04

Pitch & Crown Winners

Present live prototypes before Pine Labs engineers and faculty judges to determine the grand champions!
