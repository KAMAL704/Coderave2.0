## 🌟 About the Hackathon

Coderave 2.0 is a national-level hybrid hackathon designed to bring together innovative minds, passionate developers, designers, and problem-solvers to transform ideas into impactful technology solutions.

Organized by **Internwell SLIET Club**, this edition offers students an opportunity to demonstrate their creativity, technical expertise, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities through a competitive, time-bound development experience.

The hackathon welcomes teams from colleges and universities across India through its **Online Participation Track**, while SLIET students can participate in the **Offline Campus Track**. Shortlisted teams will present their ideas through PPT pitches, followed by an intensive **24-hour open-source development challenge** in which participants will build, test, and demonstrate their solutions.

---

## 🚀 Technology Domains

1. **Web Development** — Full-stack architectures, modern web apps (React, Next.js, Node.js), APIs, responsive design.
2. **App Development** — Cross-platform & native mobile apps (Flutter, React Native, Kotlin, Swift).
3. **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** — Generative AI, LLMs, computer vision, NLP, intelligent autonomous systems.
4. **Cybersecurity** — Defensive security, zero trust, cryptography, application vulnerability mitigation.
5. **Data Science** — Big data analytics, predictive pipelines, interactive data visualizations.
6. **Emerging Technologies** — Blockchain/Web3, IoT, cloud computing, edge computing, distributed systems.

## 🎯 Participation Tracks

### 1. Online Track — All India
- Open to eligible student teams from colleges and universities across India.
- Register online and submit your project proposal PPT.
- A maximum of **20 teams** will be shortlisted nationally for the online track.
- Shortlisted teams will pitch their PPT to the judges through an online meeting platform or offline.
- Selected teams (**only 5 teams**) will participate in the 24-hour development challenge remotely, from their preferred location.
- Finalists will submit their source code repository, project documentation, and demo video for evaluation.
- Online participants may be invited to attend the offline campus event if feasible and communicated by the organizers.
- **Prizes**: Dedicated **1st Prize Winner 🥇** and **2nd Prize Winner 🥈**.

### 2. Offline Track — SLIET Campus
- Open to eligible students of SLIET, Longowal.
- Register online and submit the project proposal PPT during registration.
- Shortlisted teams will present their PPT in person on campus on **13 February**.
- The offline 24-hour hackathon (both online and offline track) will begin at **5:00 PM on 13 February and conclude at 5:00 PM on 14 February**.
- Teams will develop and demonstrate their solutions according to the official rules and submission instructions before the grand jury.
- **Prizes**: Dedicated **1st Prize Winner 🥇**, **2nd Prize Winner 🥈**, and **3rd Prize Winner 🥉**.

## 👥 Team Formation & Eligibility Rules

- **Team Size**: Minimum 3 members, Maximum 6 members.
- **Mandatory Diversity**: **Each team must include at least 1 girl member** (Maximum 3 girl members per team).
- **Designated Team Leader**: One member must be designated as the Team Leader.
- **Registration on Behalf of Team**: **The Team Leader will complete the registration on behalf of the entire team and provide accurate details for every member.**
- **Single Team Rule**: Each participant may belong to only one registered team.
- **Eligibility**: All team members must meet the eligibility requirements announced by the organizers (active college/university students).

---

## 📋 6-Round Selection Process

- **Round 1 — Registration and PPT Submission**: The Team Leader registers the complete team and submits the project proposal PPT.
- **Round 2 — PPT Evaluation and Shortlisting**: Judges evaluate submissions based on innovation, problem relevance, feasibility, technical approach, potential impact, and presentation quality.
- **Round 3 — PPT Pitch**: Shortlisted online teams (Max 20 teams) pitch through an online meeting. Shortlisted SLIET teams pitch offline on campus on 13 February.
- **Round 4 — 24-Hour Open-Source Hackathon**: Selected teams (Both online track — top 5 teams remotely, and offline track on campus) build their solutions within the official development window (13 Feb 5:00 PM – 14 Feb 5:00 PM).
- **Round 5 — Final Submission and Evaluation**: Teams submit their working solution, source code repository, demo video, and other required deliverables. Judges evaluate the final projects.
- **Round 6 — Winner Announcement**: Official declaration of winners and awards ceremony (To be announced).

---

## ⚡ 24-Hour Open-Source Development Challenge

- Turn ideas into functional, demonstrable prototypes within 24 hours.
- Window: **13 February, 5:00 PM – 14 February, 5:00 PM**.
- Teams are expected to develop their solutions during the official event window and use open-source tools, libraries, and technologies wherever possible.
- Participants must maintain a project repository, provide clear documentation, and ensure that their work is demonstrable.
- Teams must disclose third-party components and follow applicable open-source licences.
- Previously developed work must be declared, and the core solution must comply with the organizers' originality requirements.
