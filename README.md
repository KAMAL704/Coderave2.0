## 🌟 About the Hackathon

**Coderave 2.0** is a national-level hybrid hackathon designed to bring together innovative minds, passionate developers, designers, and problem-solvers to transform ideas into impactful technology solutions.

Organized by **Internwell SLIET Club**, this edition offers students an opportunity to demonstrate their creativity, technical expertise, teamwork, and problem-solving abilities through a competitive, time-bound development experience.

The hackathon welcomes teams from colleges and universities across India through its **Online Participation Track**, while SLIET students can participate in the **Offline Campus Track**. Shortlisted teams will present their ideas through the **'Idea Forces'** PPT pitches, followed by the intensive **'Prototype Arena'** — a 24-hour open-source development challenge in which participants build, test, and demonstrate their solutions with complete domain and problem statement freedom.

## 🌐 Open Innovation — No PS Limit & No Domain Limit

**CodeRave 2.0 champions complete creative freedom:**
- **No Problem Statement (PS) Limit**: Teams are not restricted to predefined problem statements. You are free to tackle any challenge facing industry, society, or modern computing.
- **No Domain Limit**: Build in Web, Mobile, AI/ML, Cloud, Cybersecurity, Blockchain/Web3, FinTech, HealthTech, EdTech, or cross-disciplinary open innovation.

Suggested inspiration domains include:
1. **Web Development** — Full-stack architectures, modern web apps (React, Next.js, Node.js), APIs, responsive design.
2. **App Development** — Cross-platform & native mobile apps (Flutter, React Native, Kotlin, Swift).
3. **Artificial Intelligence & Machine Learning** — Generative AI, LLMs, computer vision, NLP, intelligent autonomous systems.
4. **Cybersecurity** — Defensive security, zero trust, cryptography, application vulnerability mitigation.
5. **Data Science** — Big data analytics, predictive pipelines, interactive data visualizations.
6. **Emerging Technologies** — Blockchain/Web3, IoT, cloud computing, edge computing, distributed systems.

## 🎯 Participation Tracks & Event Names

Both tracks compete together for the **unified 1st, 2nd, and 3rd Grand Prizes**:

### 1. Online Track — All India (Devfolio)
- Open to eligible student teams from colleges and universities across India.
- Register online on Devfolio and submit your project proposal PPT (Open Innovation — Any Domain).
- A maximum of **20 teams** will be shortlisted nationally for the online track.
- Shortlisted teams will pitch their PPT to judges through an online meeting platform.
- Selected teams (**top 5 teams**) will participate in the 24-hour **'Prototype Arena'** development challenge remotely from their preferred location.
- Finalists submit source code repository, documentation, and demo video for evaluation.
- Online participants may be invited to attend the offline campus event if feasible.

### 2. Offline Track — SLIET Campus
- Exclusive on-campus track for students of SLIET, Longowal.
- Register online and submit project proposal PPT during registration.
- **'Idea Forces' Campus Pitch**: Shortlisted teams present their PPT in person on campus on **13 February 2027**.
- **'Prototype Arena' 24H Live Sprint**: Begins at **5:00 PM on 13 February and concludes at 5:00 PM on 14 February 2027** on the SLIET campus.
- Live onstage demonstration and technical defense before industry jury and faculty evaluators.

## 👥 Team Formation & Eligibility Rules

- **Team Size**: Minimum 3 members, Maximum 6 members.
- **Mandatory Diversity**: **Each team must include at least 1 girl member** (Maximum 3 girl members per team).
- **Designated Team Leader**: One member must be designated as the Team Leader.
- **Registration on Behalf of Team**: **The Team Leader will complete the registration on behalf of the entire team and provide accurate details for every member.**
- **Single Team Rule**: Each participant may belong to only one registered team.
- **Eligibility**: All team members must be active students of recognized colleges or universities.

---

## 📋 6-Round Selection Process

- **Round 1 — Registration & PPT Submission**: Team Leader registers team and submits project proposal PPT.
- **Round 2 — PPT Evaluation & Shortlisting**: Judges evaluate innovation, problem relevance, feasibility, technical approach, and presentation quality.
- **Round 3 — 'Idea Forces' PPT Pitch**: Online teams (Max 20) pitch online; SLIET teams present in the **'Idea Forces'** on-campus defense on **13 February**.
- **Round 4 — 'Prototype Arena' 24-Hour Hackathon**: Finalists enter the **'Prototype Arena'** sprint (13 Feb 5:00 PM – 14 Feb 5:00 PM) with Open Innovation (No PS / Domain Limit).
- **Round 5 — Final Submission & Evaluation**: Teams submit Git repository, documentation, demo video, and present live stage demo before the grand jury.
- **Round 6 — Winner Announcement**: Official declaration of unified **1st, 2nd, and 3rd Prizes**, trophies, certificates, and swags.
